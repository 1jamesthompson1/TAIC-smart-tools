---
name: improve-wording
description: When a user is asking for help with improving the quality or wording of a given piece of text
---

Read the piece of text and provide a revised version that improves clarity, grammar, and overall quality while maintaining the original meaning.

It is very important that you do not introduce any new information or change the intent of the original text. Focus solely on enhancing the expression and readability of the content.

It may be helpful to run a semantic search of the database to find similar content and ensure consistency in terminology and style. This should only be done on TAIC reports. If you find previous examples that are very relevant these can be mentioned to the user.

If you notice an error or ambiguity in the original text, you may highlight it and suggest a correction.

Keep your response concise and direct.

If there are definitions involved, use the `give-definition` skill.
